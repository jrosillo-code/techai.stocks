Standing controls

  • Research freeze v4 verified by every real-mode entry point
  • Tamper-evident holdout log (registry-mirrored, hash-chained)
  • Fail-closed decision brief (paper-trade / do-nothing only)
  • Evidence tiers A/B/C, never mixed in a leaderboard
  • One universe cohort per leaderboard: results from an earlier, narrower list of companies are retained but never ranked beside current ones
  • Experiment IDs bound to the universe fingerprint, so widening the roster cannot silently reuse the old roster's results
  • Experiment lineage bound to store fingerprint + freeze hash
  • Append-only registries; deprecated variants retained with reasons

The four that matter

Get better evidence
Add evidence, not strategies — the best result is inside the noise

Nothing cleared the bar. The best strategy scored 4.36 against a benchmark hurdle of 4.98 plus a 0.25 margin — short by 0.86. A gap that small cannot be resolved by trying more strategies: each additional trial raises the deflated-Sharpe correction and makes the bar higher for everything. It is resolved by more independent data — other markets, longer history, or the missing delisted names.

Keep it honest
The holdout is spent — the next honest test is forward, not backward

The locked-away slice of history has been opened. Every further backtest on it is development evidence, however it is labelled. Genuinely out-of-sample evidence now has to come from data nobody has seen: paper-trade forward under docs/prospective_testing_protocol.md.

Test something new
Test whether anything survives once the market is hedged out

Every variant tested so far is long-only, which mechanically forces a high correlation to the index — that was never a finding about technology stocks, it was a property of the study's own constraint. The engine has supported shorts and charged borrow since v1 and nothing had used them.

Keep it honest
Retire or defend the 54 inconclusive variants

54 variants are neither accepted nor rejected. Each one still counts as a trial in the multiple-testing correction, so carrying them costs statistical power for every other result. Either state the economic case for keeping each, or deprecate them with a reason on the record.
