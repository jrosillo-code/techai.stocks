Standing controls

  • Research freeze v3 verified by every real-mode entry point
  • Tamper-evident holdout log (registry-mirrored, hash-chained)
  • Fail-closed decision brief (paper-trade / do-nothing only)
  • Evidence tiers A/B/C, never mixed in a leaderboard
  • One universe cohort per leaderboard: results from an earlier, narrower list of companies are retained but never ranked beside current ones
  • Experiment IDs bound to the universe fingerprint, so widening the roster cannot silently reuse the old roster's results
  • Experiment lineage bound to store fingerprint + freeze hash
  • Append-only registries; deprecated variants retained with reasons
